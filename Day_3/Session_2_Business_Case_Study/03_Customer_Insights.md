# 👥 Customer Insights & Segmentation

> **Learning Goal:** Learn how to group customers into segments to provide personalized experiences and increase loyalty.

---

## 🧐 What is Customer Segmentation?

Not all customers are the same. Some buy once a year, others buy every week. **Segmentation** is the process of dividing a customer base into groups of individuals that are similar in specific ways.

---

## 1️⃣ Demographic Segmentation

Dividing the market by personal characteristics.
- **Age**: Do 18–25 year olds buy differently than 45–60 year olds? (e.g., Electronics vs. Household Food).
- **Location**: Do Mumbai customers spend more than Chennai customers?

---

## 2️⃣ Behavioral Segmentation (The Gold Mine)

Dividing customers based on their actions.
- **Member vs. Non-Member**: Premium members usually have higher AOV and frequency.
- **Payment Method**: Do UPI users buy more frequently than Cash users?

---

## 3️⃣ RFM Analysis (Mini-Introduction)

Professional analysts use the **RFM Model**:
- **Recency**: How recently did they buy?
- **Frequency**: How often do they buy?
- **Monetary**: How much total money have they spent?

---

## 💡 Industry Case: The "Dormant Member"

Through data, you find a group of customers who:
1. Are **Premium Members**.
2. Have high **Monetary** value in the past.
3. Have **Recency** of > 90 days (haven't visited in 3 months).

**Insight**: These are your VIPs who are about to leave (churn).
**Action**: Send them a "We Miss You" coupon for ₹500 off.

---
