# 📈 KPI Identification in Retail

> **Learning Goal:** Learn how to translate raw data into "Key Performance Indicators" (KPIs) that business leaders care about.

---

## 🧐 What is a KPI?

A **Key Performance Indicator (KPI)** is a measurable value that demonstrates how effectively a company is achieving key business objectives.

If a dataset is the "Story," KPIs are the "Headline."

---

## 🏆 Top Retail KPIs

For a company like SuperMart, these are the 4 most critical metrics:

### 1. Total Revenue (Sales)
- **What it is**: The total money brought in by sales.
- **Formula**: `SUM(Amount)`
- **Goal**: High growth over time.

### 2. Average Order Value (AOV)
- **What it is**: The average amount spent by a customer per visit.
- **Formula**: `Total Revenue / Total Transaction Count`
- **Goal**: Cross-selling (e.g., "Do you want fries with that?") increases AOV.

### 3. Basket Size (UuP - Units per Purchase)
- **What it is**: The average number of items per transaction.
- **Formula**: `Total Items Sold / Total Transaction Count`
- **Goal**: Getting customers to buy more variety.

### 4. Sales Growth Rate
- **What it is**: How much more/less are we selling compared to last month?
- **Formula**: `(Current Month Revenue - Last Month Revenue) / Last Month Revenue * 100`

---

## 💡 Industry context: The "Why" behind KPIs

Imagine Store A has high **Revenue** but low **AOV**.
- **Insight**: Many people visit, but buy small/cheap items.
- **Action**: Launch "Buy 2 Get 1" offers or bundle high-value products.

Imagine Store B has high **AOV** but low **Revenue**.
- **Insight**: High-value customers visit, but very few of them.
- **Action**: Increase marketing and footfall to bring in more people.
