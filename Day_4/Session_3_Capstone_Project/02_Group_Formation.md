# Capstone Project: Group Formation

## Overview
Data Analytics is rarely a solo endeavor. In industry, you will work closely with Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Product Managers, and Business Stakeholders. The capstone project simulates this collaborative environment.

## Group Structure
- **Size:** Teams will consist of 3-4 members.
- **Diversity of Skills:** We encourage forming teams with complementary skills. A strong team often includes:
  - Someone comfortable with SQL/Python (Data Prep/Analysis).
  - Someone with a good eye for design and Tableau/Power BI (Visualization).
  - Someone with strong communication and business acumen (Storytelling/Presentation).

## Roles & Responsibilities
While everyone is expected to contribute to all phases, assigning primary roles helps ensure accountability:

1. **Project Lead / Scrum Master:**
   - Keeps the team on track with deadlines.
   - Organizes meetings and manages the Kanban board or task list.
   - Ensures communication flows smoothly.

2. **Data Lead (SQL/Python):**
   - Primary owner of data extraction, cleaning, and transformation.
   - Ensures data integrity before it reaches the dashboard.

3. **Visualization Lead (Power BI/Tableau):**
   - Primary owner of dashboard architecture and design.
   - Ensures the dashboard is interactive, accurate, and user-friendly.

4. **Business & Strategy Lead:**
   - Owns the narrative. Ensures the analysis actually answers the business problem.
   - Takes the lead on writing the final report and crafting the presentation deck.

## Formation Process
1. **Networking:** Use the next 30 minutes to discuss skills and interests with your peers.
2. **Team Registration:** Once you have a group of 3-4, register your team name and member list with the instructor.
3. **Solo Option:** If you strongly prefer working alone, please consult with the instructor. Note that solo projects require the same level of deliverables as group projects.

## Collaboration Tools
Set up your team workspace immediately:
- **Communication:** Create a Slack/Discord channel or a WhatsApp group.
- **Code Sharing:** Set up a shared GitHub repository.
- **File Sharing:** Create a shared Google Drive or OneDrive folder for datasets, assets, and presentation drafts.
