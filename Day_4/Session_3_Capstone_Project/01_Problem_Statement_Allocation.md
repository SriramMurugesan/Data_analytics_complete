# Capstone Project: Problem Statement Allocation

## Overview
The capstone project is the culmination of the Data Analytics curriculum. It is designed to test your ability to apply SQL, Python, Business Analysis, and Data Visualization skills to solve a real-world business problem end-to-end.

## The Objective
You are tasked with acting as a Data Analyst for a fictional, mid-sized company facing strategic challenges. You must analyze their raw data, identify underlying issues or opportunities, and present actionable recommendations supported by a dynamic dashboard.

## Available Problem Statements

Depending on the course structure, you will either be assigned or get to choose from the following industry domains:

### Domain 1: E-commerce Retail Analytics
- **The Client:** An online retailer experiencing stagnating sales and high customer churn.
- **The Problem:** The marketing team needs to know which customer segments are most valuable, which products drive the highest profit margins, and where marketing spend should be focused next quarter.
- **Key Deliverables:** Customer segmentation model, sales trend analysis, inventory performance metrics.

### Domain 2: Healthcare Operations
- **The Client:** A regional hospital network dealing with resource allocation issues and long patient wait times.
- **The Problem:** Hospital administrators need to understand peak admission times, patient readmission rates, and staff utilization to optimize scheduling and improve patient outcomes.
- **Key Deliverables:** Admissions forecasting, readmission risk factors, resource allocation dashboard.

### Domain 3: Financial Risk & Fraud Detection
- **The Client:** A mid-sized retail bank launching a new credit card product.
- **The Problem:** The risk management team needs a system to identify potentially fraudulent transactions in real-time and assess the creditworthiness of new applicants based on historical data.
- **Key Deliverables:** Fraud detection rules/patterns, applicant risk scoring model, transaction monitoring dashboard.

## Selection Process
1. **Review:** Read through the problem statements carefully. Consider which domain aligns best with your interests or career goals.
2. **Submit:** If choosing, submit your top 2 preferences to the instructor.
3. **Allocation:** Final project assignments will be distributed by the end of today's session.
