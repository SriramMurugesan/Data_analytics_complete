# Capstone Completion: Dashboard Finalization

## Overview
As the capstone project nears its end, the focus shifts from data manipulation and exploration to the refinement of your final deliverables. The dashboard is often the primary touchpoint for stakeholders, so it must be polished, accurate, and intuitive.

## Key Steps for Finalization

### 1. UX/UI Review
- **Alignment and Spacing:** Ensure all charts, slicers, and text boxes are perfectly aligned. Use the grid/snap tools in Power BI or Tableau to maintain equal padding.
- **Color Palette consistency:** Verify that your color choices are consistent (e.g., if Revenue is green in one chart, it should be green everywhere). Ensure colors are accessible to colorblind users.
- **Clutter Reduction:** Remove unnecessary axis titles, gridlines, or legends if the data labels or titles already explain them. 

### 2. Interactivity Check
- **Slicer Syncing:** If you have multiple pages, check if your slicers properly sync across them (if intended).
- **Cross-Filtering:** Click through various bars and data points. Does the cross-filtering behave as expected, or does it make other charts unreadable? Adjust "Edit Interactions" as necessary.
- **Tooltips:** Hover over data points. Ensure tooltips are formatted correctly (e.g., currency symbols, correct decimal places) and only show relevant information.

### 3. Performance Optimization
- **Load Time:** Does the dashboard take too long to load or filter? 
- **Unused Fields/Tables:** Remove any tables or calculated columns from the data model that are not being used in the final report to reduce file size and improve speed.

## Final Sign-Off Checklist
- [ ] Are all KPIs calculating correctly? (Spot-check the math).
- [ ] Is there a clear, descriptive title for the dashboard and every visual?
- [ ] Are date formats standard and readable?
- [ ] Are navigation buttons (if any) working?
