# Valedictory Ceremony: Feedback Collection

## Overview
Just as data drives business decisions, feedback drives the improvement of this program. We ask that all participants complete a final program evaluation.

## Why Your Feedback Matters
- **Curriculum Updates:** If certain topics felt rushed or unnecessary, your feedback helps us adjust the pacing for the next cohort.
- **Instructor Evaluation:** Helps instructors understand what teaching methods were most effective and where they can improve.
- **Tool Relevance:** The industry moves fast. If you felt a certain tool (e.g., a specific Python library) was less relevant, let us know so we can update the syllabus.

## The Feedback Form
You will receive a link to an anonymous online survey. Please be honest and constructive. Key areas covered will include:
1. **Course Content:** Was the material appropriately challenging? Did it meet your expectations?
2. **Instruction:** Were the concepts explained clearly? Were the labs helpful?
3. **Pacing:** Was the program too fast, too slow, or just right?
4. **Capstone Experience:** Did the final project accurately reflect real-world scenarios?
5. **Facilities/Platform:** (If applicable) Were the learning platforms and resources adequate?

*Please take 10 minutes to complete this form before leaving the final session.*
