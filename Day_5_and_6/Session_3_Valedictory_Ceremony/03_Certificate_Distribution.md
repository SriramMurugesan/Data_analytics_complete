# Valedictory Ceremony: Certificate Distribution

## Overview
The final act of the program is to recognize the hard work and dedication of the participants. Successfully completing a rigorous Data Analytics bootcamp is a significant achievement.

## Certification Criteria
To receive the final certificate of completion, participants must have met the following criteria throughout the program:
- **Attendance:** Maintained the required attendance percentage across all sessions.
- **Lab Completion:** Successfully submitted the required daily lab assignments.
- **Capstone Project:** Actively participated in the capstone project and final presentation.
- **Evaluation:** Achieved a passing score based on the jury evaluation and overall continuous assessment.

## Next Steps: Leveraging Your Certificate
Getting the certificate is just the beginning. Here is how you should use it:
1. **LinkedIn Profile:** Add the certification to your LinkedIn profile. Post about your capstone project, tagging your teammates and instructors. Share screenshots of your final dashboard.
2. **Resume Update:** Update your resume with the specific technical skills you acquired (Python, Pandas, SQL, Power BI/Tableau) and list your capstone project under an "Academic Projects" or "Portfolio" section.
3. **GitHub Portfolio:** Ensure your capstone code and documentation are cleanly organized in a public GitHub repository. This serves as tangible proof of your skills for potential employers.

*Congratulations to all participants on successfully completing the Applied Data Analytics and Visualization program!*
