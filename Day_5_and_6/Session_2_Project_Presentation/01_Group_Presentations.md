# Project Presentation: Group Presentations

## Overview
The group presentation is your opportunity to showcase the hard work, technical skills, and business acumen your team has developed throughout the program. This session simulates a real-world stakeholder meeting where you must pitch your findings and justify your recommendations.

## Presentation Format & Logistics
- **Time Limit:** Each group will have exactly 15 minutes to present, followed by 5 minutes of Q&A. Time limits will be strictly enforced.
- **Participation:** Every team member must speak for at least 3 minutes during the presentation.
- **Deliverables Required:**
  - Slide Deck (PowerPoint / Keynote / Google Slides).
  - Live Dashboard demonstration.
  - Final Documentation (submitted to the instructor prior to presenting).

## Structuring the Presentation
A winning presentation tells a compelling data story. Follow this structure:

1. **Introduction (2 mins):** Introduce the team, the client (your problem domain), and the core business problem you set out to solve.
2. **Methodology & Data (3 mins):** Briefly explain where the data came from, any major cleaning steps, and the architecture of your solution (keep it high-level; save deep technical details for the Q&A if asked).
3. **Insights & Dashboard Demo (6 mins):** 
   - This is the core of the presentation. 
   - State 3 major insights. 
   - Transition to the live dashboard to visually prove these insights. Show, don't just tell. Demonstrate the interactivity.
4. **Recommendations (3 mins):** Based on your insights, what should the business do next? Make the recommendations specific, measurable, and actionable.
5. **Conclusion & Future Scope (1 min):** Wrap up the presentation and mention what you would do if you had another month to work on this data.

## Presentation Best Practices
- **Know Your Audience:** You are presenting to "Business Executives", not other data analysts. Avoid overly technical jargon (e.g., don't explain how a LEFT JOIN works, explain what the resulting data means for the business).
- **Body Language & Delivery:** Stand up straight, make eye contact, and project your voice. Avoid reading directly from the slides.
- **Handling Glitches:** If the live dashboard fails to load or errors out during the demo, don't panic. Have screenshots of your dashboard in the appendix of your slide deck as a backup.
